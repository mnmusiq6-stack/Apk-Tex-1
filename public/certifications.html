Certifications & Compliance

Compliance built for international sourcing.

We are building the compliance framework required to work with international apparel buyers. Only issued certificates and completed audits will be represented as active credentials.

IEC

Import Export Code for export activity. Status: application / verification as applicable.

SEDEX / SMETA

Social-compliance pathway for buyer onboarding. SMETA is an audit methodology, not a certification.

WRAP

Apparel-focused social compliance certification pathway.

ISO 9001

Quality-management certification pathway for structured production and quality processes.

Do not treat a roadmap item as a certificate. APK TEX will publish certificate numbers, issue dates and verification links only after they are actually available.
