About APK TEX

A practical manufacturing partner for international buyers.

APK TEX is a Tiruppur-based garment manufacturer focused on knitted apparel, OEM production and private-label manufacturing.

We support product development from sampling and material selection through cutting, sewing, quality control, finishing and packing. Our focus is clear communication, consistent workmanship and practical execution.

For international buyers, the goal is straightforward: understand the product, confirm specifications, develop the sample, quote transparently and execute the approved production.
