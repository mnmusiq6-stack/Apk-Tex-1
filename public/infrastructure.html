Inside the workflow

A factory story buyers can understand.

APK TEX is focused on knitted apparel manufacturing in Tiruppur. We structure the production journey so a buyer can move from a product idea or tech pack to an approved sample, production, inspection and packing.

01 · Product development

Sampling & development

Samples are developed against the buyer's reference, measurements, fabric direction, construction and finishing requirements. The objective is to confirm the product before bulk production.

02 · Preparation

Fabric & cutting

Approved materials are prepared for production and panels are cut according to the required pattern and specifications.

03 · Production

Sewing operations

Garment panels move through sewing according to the approved construction, measurements and production instructions.

04 · Control

Quality inspection

Measurements, stitching, appearance and finishing are checked against the approved requirement through the workflow.

05 · Presentation

Finishing & pressing

Garments move through thread trimming, pressing, folding and presentation preparation after inspection.

06 · Dispatch preparation

Packing & documentation

Packing is organized around buyer requirements, including presentation, size/quantity grouping and shipment-ready preparation.
