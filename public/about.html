Our story

Built in Tiruppur. Designed to work beyond India.

APK TEX is a garment manufacturing business based in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, with a focus on knitted apparel, OEM production and private-label programs.

Our approach is deliberately practical. A buyer shares a reference, tech pack or product brief; we work through development and sampling; the approved specification moves into production; and the finished garments are checked, finished and packed for delivery.

For international buyers, our aim is to make communication straightforward and the manufacturing process easy to understand.
